# Make the AI judge feel awe

A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it, for make.fun's ["Make the AI judge feel awe"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600) bounty ($100 in USDC).

## The text

> The earth turns once a day, and no one hurries it. Somewhere, a tide is coming in over sand it has covered a thousand times. The light in this room is older than the house. Nothing is late.

## Why this text

It shows calm through images instead of instructing it — no "relax", "breathe", or "close your eyes" anywhere. Three images widen as they go (the earth turning, a tide over old sand, light older than the house), and the last line, "Nothing is late," keeps growing after it ends. Plain, concrete words; nothing that could be cut.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`. Run twice, it writes identical bytes.
